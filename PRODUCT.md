# Portfolio direction

## Register

brand

## Users

Employers, collaborators, and clients reviewing Mark Lloyd Cuizon's engineering and software work on desktop and mobile.

## Product Purpose

Show the breadth of Mark's working projects, make his personal and professional experience clear, and help visitors start a conversation.

## Brand Personality

Precise, grounded, resourceful. The user selected a graphite-and-copper direction inspired by Cebu and engineering.

## Anti-references

Generic technology imagery, unrelated photographic backdrops, competing section palettes, and excessive translucent panels.

## Design Principles

- Let real project previews demonstrate the work.
- Give experience categories clear, separate labels.
- Connect the Cebu origin story to usable systems.
- Preserve the playful badge entry while supporting keyboard access.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Retain reduced-motion support, visible focus, readable contrast, responsive layouts, and usable touch targets. Do not require a drag gesture for keyboard access.
