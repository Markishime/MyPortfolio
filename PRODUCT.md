# Mark Lloyd Cuizon — Cinematic Portfolio

## Register
brand

## Product Purpose
A personal film of systems. Hiring engineers and founders scroll a 2.5D journey through two years of work that binds hardware, software, and AI.

## Users
- Hiring managers and founders evaluating a computer engineer
- Technical peers scanning shipped systems
- Anyone arriving from GitHub or LinkedIn on a phone or laptop

## Scene
Night desk in Cebu. Humid air, one phosphor lamp, a portrait that the camera pushes into. The page is a reel, not a card grid.

## Brand voice
Humid. Engineered. Phosphor.

## Anti-references
- Generic neon-cyber SaaS template
- Centered icon-title-subtitle cards
- Inter / Outfit / Space Grotesk landing pages
- AI purple-pink gradients
- WebGL hero that janks on a mid-range Android

## Principles
- Scroll is the camera. One idea per fold.
- Depth is CSS + GSAP, not a 3D engine.
- Desktop gets the long take. Phones get the same story, fewer moving layers.
- Experience is two years. Say it plainly.
